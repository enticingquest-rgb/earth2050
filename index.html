CLASSIFIED 2050 REPORT

SUBJECT: SYNTHETIC CARBON CAPTURE

Directive Summary: Traditional afforestation parameters have proven insufficient for rapid atmospheric stabilization. The Earth 2050 initiative relies heavily on Direct Air Capture (DAC) utilizing advanced amine-functionalized Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs).

CHEMISTRY MECHANISM

MOFs act as molecular sponges. The amine groups chemically bind to CO₂ molecules from the ambient air at concentrations as low as 400ppm, physically separating them from unreactive N₂ and O₂.

Once captured, thermal shifting (heating the MOFs) releases pure CO₂. This byproduct is then combined with green hydrogen (derived via solar electrolysis) to synthesize sustainable aviation fuels and carbon-negative plastics, effectively closing the carbon loop globally.
